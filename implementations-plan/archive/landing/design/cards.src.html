Unleashed

Privacy nobody can switch off, tested in the open.

Unleashed is a lab for private apps on Aztec. Every experiment is a working app with its source in the open, built to find out what unstoppable privacy can do today.

What every screen keeps

  • %%I24:check%%Reduced motion shows a still frame. Pause motion stops every screen on the page, not only the background.

My pick · moderate confidence

Strip, Portal, Drip and Tuner.

The strip keeps the row's text where the landing board put it and gives every engine the full width, which the bridge lanes need on a phone. Portal is the one you already liked inside option B, and it is the clearest picture of public in, private out. Drip shows the faucet's whole job in one loop: a tap, two tokens, into an account. Tuner keeps the open slot alive without inventing an experiment.

The strongest case against: Both ways is the more accurate bridge, since the app sends and exits, and Off air is the more honest empty slot. If you would rather be exact than calm, take B2 and N2.
