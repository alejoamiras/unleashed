Fix: `_bind` picks its consumption secret by the publish flag — `REGISTER_SECRET` (0) for `register_*`, `BIND_HARNESS_SECRET` (0xB1AD, in `register_hash_lib`) for `bind_*` — so a factory leaf (committed to `compute_secret_hash([0])`) is unconsumable through the harness path; the harness injects harness-secret messages. ## Notes

- **Never type-check the hub crate with `nargo compile`**: it overwrites the committed TRANSPILED artifact with a plain one and the TXE runner then fails every test with "public bytecode has not been transpiled". Use `nargo-5.sh <crate> check`, or restore the artifact from git (`git checkout -- …/target/*.json`) afterwards. (Bit once here.)
- "No burn persists" on a paused exit is atomicity: a `should_fail_with` test cannot observe state after the panic, so the property is shown by `exits_resume_after_unpause` (balance intact after a pause/unpause cycle) rather than after the refused call.
- `add_public_authwit_from_call` works with `create_light_account`; the private authwit needs `create_contract_account` (a SchnorrAccount that can answer `is_valid`).
- A `global` initializer may call `EthAddress::from_field` without the `FromField` trait in scope; function bodies cannot.
